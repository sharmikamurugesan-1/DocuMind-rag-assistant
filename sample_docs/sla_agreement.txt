MASTER SERVICE & CLOUD SLA AGREEMENT
Effective Date: January 1, 2025
Parties: Global Tech Provider Inc. ("Provider") and Enterprise Client Corp. ("Client")

SECTION 1: SCOPE OF SERVICES
Provider agrees to deliver 99.9% uptime for cloud hosting and managed database services. Planned maintenance windows shall occur on Sundays between 02:00 UTC and 04:00 UTC with 72 hours prior notice.

SECTION 2: REFUND POLICY & SLA CREDITS
If monthly availability drops below 99.9%, Client is eligible for Service Credits:
- 99.0% to 99.8%: 10% credit of monthly billing.
- 95.0% to 98.9%: 25% credit of monthly billing.
- Below 95.0%: 50% credit of monthly billing.

SECTION 3: CONFIDENTIALITY & DATA PROTECTION
All customer proprietary information, transaction databases, and source code shall remain strictly confidential. Provider agrees to comply with GDPR, SOC-2, and standard ISO 27001 data isolation policies.

SECTION 4: TERMINATION & PENALTY CLAUSES
Clause 4.1: Either party may terminate with 30 days written notice.
Clause 4.2: In the event of late service delivery or milestone failure exceeding 14 business days, Provider shall incur a penalty fee of 1.5% of total contract value per week of delay, capped at 15% maximum aggregate liability.
